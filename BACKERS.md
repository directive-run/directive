## How to be listed

1. **Sponsor on GitHub** at any tier via [github.com/sponsors/directive-run](https://github.com/sponsors/directive-run).
2. **Receive the tier welcome message** with instructions on how to confirm your display preferences (name, logo, link target, or anonymous).
3. **Reply to the welcome message** with your preferences. Listings are added within a few business days.

To update or remove a listing, open an issue on the [main repository](https://github.com/directive-run/directive/issues) or email the maintainer at the address provided in your welcome message.

## A note on transparency

Sponsorship dollars fund:

- Dedicated development time on the library, its 16 packages, and the documentation site
- Infrastructure (CI minutes, npm publish, domain renewal)
- Roadmap work for v2 (signed audit chain, multi-tenant audit queries, additional framework adapters)

The project is zero-VC, zero-foundation, zero-employer-subsidy. Every sponsor matters.
